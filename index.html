About me

I am a currently a post-doctoral fellow at the University of Auckland. Previously, I was a PIMS-CNRS post-doctoral fellow at the University of Regina working under the supervision of Karen Meagher. In October 2024, I completed my PhD at the University of Ottawa under the supervision of Mateja Šajna. I am predominantly interested in graph decomposition problems with particular emphasis on cycle decomposition problems. I am also interested in pursuit-evasion games played on graphs.
